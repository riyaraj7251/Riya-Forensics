## 🔬 About me

I'm a forensic scientist and educator working where **environmental science, biology and spatial data meet the crime scene**. My research asks how traces like soil, microbes, pesticides and biological fluids can link people, objects and bodies to places and timelines.

Alongside research, I teach forensic science, coordinate undergraduate research projects, and mentor students through their first publications.

## 🧭 Research interests

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| 🌍 **Environmental & soil forensics** | Soil provenance, pesticide contamination, geo-referenced evidence |
| 🦠 **Forensic microbiology** | Microbiome signatures for provenance and post-mortem interval estimation |
| ☠️ **Forensic toxicology** | Pesticide and pyrethroid poisoning trends in India |
| 🩸 **Biological evidence** | Bloodstain pattern analysis, blood detection, DNA from hard tissues |
| 🗺️ **Spatial forensics & criminology** | Crime mapping, GIS, remote sensing, cyber criminology |
| 🥗 **Forensic nutrition** | Biomarker-based nutrition profiling |

## 🚧 Current work

- **Doctoral research — Forensic soil provenance in Bengaluru.** Building a geo-referenced reference dataset of soil mineralogy and microbiome profiles across the city, aimed at linking evidence to its place of origin.
- **Bengaluru Forensic Atlas (GIS).** An interactive map that brings together forensically relevant spatial data for Bengaluru. The first layer covers adulterated spices, with soil and more to follow.
- **Textbook of Forensic Biology and Serology.** An edited volume of student-written chapters.
